How Blood Pressure Works

A comprehensive system designed for effective blood pressure management

1. Record Your Reading

Camera Scan AI: Simply point your camera at your blood pressure monitor's display and let AI automatically extract systolic, diastolic, and pulse values. No typing needed - the app reads the numbers directly from OMRON, Microlife, Beurer, and most digital monitors.

Manual Entry: Prefer to type? Enter values manually with a streamlined interface. Takes just seconds to log a reading.

Rich Context: Add detailed notes about what you were doing - meals eaten, medications taken, exercise, stress levels, sleep quality, or any factors that might affect your blood pressure. You can attach up to 8 photos to a reading, and find any entry again later straight from iOS Spotlight search.

2. Track Patterns

Calendar View: See all your readings in an intuitive calendar layout. Each day shows your measurements with color-coded indicators for low, normal, elevated, high, or very high readings - based on widely used guidelines, or on the ranges you set yourself.

Automatic Statistics: Get instant averages over any date range, your lowest and highest readings, and a breakdown of how many readings fell into each range - no math required.

Visual Charts: Separate systolic, diastolic, MAP, and pulse charts show your trends over days, weeks, months, or any custom date range, each with your target value drawn as a reference line.

3. Get AI Insights

Pattern Detection: AI analyzes your readings and notes to identify correlations. Discover what activities, foods, or situations consistently raise or lower your blood pressure.

Personalized Recommendations: Get specific insights based on your data. The AI might notice patterns like "Your BP tends to be higher on days when you mention 'stressed at work'" or "Readings are lower on days with morning exercise."

Chat with Your Data: Ask questions in natural language like "What was my average BP in the evenings last month?" or "Show me readings after I took my medication." The AI understands context and gives you instant answers.

Free every day: Ask a Question, Quick Chat, Voice Summary, and a Quick Health Report are included for everyone, with a generous daily allowance. The deepest reports - Deep Research, Pattern Detective, Doctor Summary, the Pro MAP Report - come with the optional Pro subscription.

4. Share with Your Doctor

Professional Reports: Export comprehensive PDF, CSV, Excel, or JSON reports formatted for medical review. Reports include summary statistics, trend charts, and all your notes - everything your doctor needs in one document.

Apple Health Integration: Automatically sync all readings to Apple Health. Import historical data from other apps or devices. Your blood pressure data stays connected with your complete health picture.

Cloud Backup: All data is automatically backed up to your private iCloud account. Switch devices seamlessly - your entire blood pressure history moves with you, encrypted and secure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal blood pressure reading?

According to medical guidelines, normal blood pressure is typically below 120/80 mmHg. Readings between 120-129 systolic and below 80 diastolic are considered elevated. Values of 130/80 or higher may indicate hypertension. However, target ranges can vary by age and health conditions, so consult your doctor for personalized guidance. Feeltracker Blood Pressure color-codes every reading against these ranges, and you can adjust the thresholds yourself if your doctor has given you different targets.

How does Camera Scan AI work?

Camera Scan AI reads the numbers straight off your blood pressure monitor's screen: point your iPhone or iPad camera at the display, and Feeltracker Blood Pressure fills in the systolic, diastolic, and pulse fields for you. There's no pairing and no brand-specific setup — it works with most digital monitors, including OMRON, Microlife, and Beurer. Scanning is free for everyone, with a generous daily allowance, and it takes a couple of seconds instead of typing three numbers by hand.

What is MAP and should I track it?

MAP (Mean Arterial Pressure) is a calculated value that represents the average blood pressure in your arteries during one cardiac cycle. It's calculated as: MAP = (2 × Diastolic + Systolic) ÷ 3. Some doctors find MAP useful for assessing cardiovascular risk. Feeltracker Blood Pressure works it out automatically for every reading, charts it alongside your systolic and diastolic values, and can export a MAP PDF report split into morning, evening, and full-day views. You can also set your own MAP bands, with an LVAD focus option for people tracking with a heart pump.

Can I import my old blood pressure data?

Yes — Feeltracker Blood Pressure can import your existing history from Apple Health or from a spreadsheet. If you've been tracking in Apple Health, the app shows you the readings it found and imports the ones you select, and you can switch on automatic import so new Health readings come across on their own. If your history lives in a spreadsheet, download the app's Excel template, paste your data into it, and import the file. JSON files and ZIP backups from another device work too.

How often should I check my blood pressure?

Home monitoring guidance generally suggests two readings a day — one in the morning before medication and one in the evening — taken at consistent times, though your doctor's advice for your situation always comes first. Feeltracker Blood Pressure lets you log as many readings a day as you like and set up to four reminders, each on the days of the week you choose, so a twice-daily routine is easy to keep.

Is my blood pressure data private?

Your readings live on your device and in your own private iCloud account — Feeltracker Blood Pressure has no account to create, no email address to hand over, and no copy of your history on our servers. The one exception is the AI features: when you ask the AI something, the readings needed to answer travel through our own relay server to the AI provider so we can answer you. That relay stores nothing — no request or reply is ever written to disk — and you can switch the AI to on-device Apple Intelligence, or turn it off entirely, in the app's AI settings. Everything else, including Apple Health sync, stays between your devices and Apple.

Can I track blood pressure for family members?

Feeltracker Blood Pressure is built for one person's readings, so each family member is best tracking on their own device with their own Apple ID and iCloud account. That keeps everyone's history, charts, and reports separate, and means each person's data syncs only to their own devices.

Do you show ads or track me?

No. We've completely removed all advertisements from the app. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data to advertisers.

Your data syncs via iCloud - just like Apple Notes and Photos. This means we don't need to know your email address, name, or any personal information. Your blood pressure data is stored on your devices and in your iCloud account, and it's completely private whether you use the free version or premium.

Do I need a subscription?

No subscription is required. The app is free to download, all tracking, charts, Apple Health sync, backups, and exports are free, and every user gets real AI features free every day — Ask a Question, Quick Chat, Voice Summary, a Quick Health Report, Make Me Laugh, Calm Me Down, and Camera Scan AI — with no ads, ever. An optional Pro subscription unlocks the most in-depth AI features (Deep Research, Doctor Summary, Pattern Detective, the Pro MAP Report, Pro Chat, and more), runs them on our most capable AI, and raises the generous daily limits.

Does it work with my OMRON, Microlife, or Beurer monitor?

Feeltracker Blood Pressure works with any home blood pressure monitor, because it reads the numbers rather than connecting to the device. Point your camera at the display and Camera Scan AI fills in systolic, diastolic, and pulse — that works with most digital monitors, including OMRON, Microlife, and Beurer — or type the values in yourself with the pickers or the keyboard. There's no Bluetooth pairing, no compatibility list, and nothing to break when you replace your monitor.

How do I share my blood pressure readings with my doctor?

Feeltracker Blood Pressure can produce a doctor-ready PDF of any date range in a few taps, and you can email it, message it, print it, or AirDrop it straight from the app. PDF, CSV, Excel, and JSON exports are all free, as is the dedicated MAP report that splits your Mean Arterial Pressure into morning, evening, and full-day views for a month, quarter, year, or custom range. With Pro, the AI can also write a plain-language Doctor Summary of the same period to go with the numbers.

Can I set my own blood pressure targets and ranges?

Yes — Feeltracker Blood Pressure lets you set target systolic, diastolic, and pulse values, which appear as reference lines on your charts, and edit the thresholds for every range band (Low, Normal, Elevated, High, Very High) so they match the guidance you were actually given. Your charts, calendar colors, and PDF reports all follow the ranges you set, not just the defaults. MAP has its own adjustable bands, including an LVAD focus option.

Does Feeltracker Blood Pressure work on iPad and Mac?

Yes — one download covers iPhone and iPad, and it also runs on Apple Silicon Macs through the Mac App Store. Your readings sync automatically between all of them through your own private iCloud account, so a reading logged on your iPhone is on your iPad moments later. There's a home-screen widget showing your recent averages, and entries are indexed in Spotlight so you can search your history from anywhere in iOS.
