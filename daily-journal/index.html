How Daily Journal Works

Your personal journaling companion for capturing life's moments and discovering patterns

1. Write Your Thoughts

Instant Entry: Open the app and start writing immediately. No setup, no configuration, no complexity. Just your thoughts and the page. The clean, distraction-free interface helps you focus on expressing yourself.

Rich Media: Enhance entries with photos — attach up to 8 images to capture memories visually, straight from your camera or library, or let AI scan items and text into your entry. Every entry becomes a complete snapshot of your day.

Note Templates: Use pre-made templates for daily reflections, gratitude journaling, goal tracking, dream logs, or create your own custom templates. Templates provide structure while still giving you freedom to write naturally.

2. See Patterns Emerge

Sentiment Gauge: As you write, an optional color-coded gauge rates the emotional tone of the entry, from negative through neutral to positive. Pro adds a Sentiment Timeline that charts how that tone moves across weeks and months.

Word Frequency: Discover which topics, people, or themes you write about most. A word cloud and a ranked frequency list reveal what's consistently on your mind, and tapping a word opens every entry that uses it.

Year, Month, and Day Views: Browse your whole journaling history, from a year heatmap of the days you wrote down to a single day. Each day lists your entries in full, with a record and word count, so you can jump straight to what you were thinking on any date.

3. Reflect and Grow

AI-Powered Insights: Ask questions about your journal: "What were my happiest days last month?" or "Show me all entries where I mentioned my goals." Get instant, thoughtful answers that help you understand your journey.

Personal Growth Tracking: Review how your thoughts, concerns, and aspirations evolve. See progress toward goals mentioned in past entries. Identify growth areas and celebrate how far you've come.

Search Everything: Search any word across years of journaling, narrow it to a date range, and jump straight to the matching entries. Your journal is also indexed in iOS Spotlight, so a search from your Home Screen finds it too.

4. Keep Everything Safe

Private & Secure: Your journal lives on your device and in your personal iCloud account — we have no servers storing it, we never access it, and we never sell it. Your words stay yours.

Automatic Backup: Weekly automatic backups (a ZIP of your entries and photos) ensure you never lose your thoughts. Manual backup anytime to export your entire journal. Your words are protected against device loss or failure.

Cross-Device Sync: Write on iPhone, continue on iPad, review on Mac. All your entries sync automatically via iCloud. Your journal follows you everywhere, always up-to-date on every device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my journal private?

Yes — Feeltracker Daily Journal keeps your entries on your device and in your own private iCloud account, so we have no servers holding your journal and nobody at Feeltracker can read what you write. There are no ads and no cross-app tracking. Optional AI features send the entries you ask about to a privacy-respecting cloud service, and you can switch AI off at any time; with AI off, the sentiment gauge runs entirely on-device using Apple's Natural Language framework, and with the Apple Intelligence option every AI feature runs on-device.

How does sentiment analysis work?

Feeltracker Daily Journal scores the emotional tone of each entry as you write and shows it as a color-coded gauge under the text, running from red (negative) through gray (neutral) to green and blue (positive). The gauge is optional and can be turned off in Settings. By default it uses the app's privacy-respecting AI; if you turn AI off, it switches to Apple's built-in on-device Natural Language framework instead.

What is word frequency analysis?

Word frequency analysis in Feeltracker Daily Journal counts the words across all your entries and ranks the ones you use most, so the people, places, and worries that keep coming up become visible. Common filler words are filtered out in your language, the same data is also shown as a word cloud, and tapping any word opens every entry that contains it.

Can I add photos to my journal?

Yes! You can attach up to 8 photos to each journal entry. Photos are stored efficiently with automatic thumbnail generation, and they sync across all your devices via iCloud.

What are note templates?

Templates are pre-written prompts that help you start writing quickly. Create custom templates for things like gratitude lists, daily reviews, goal tracking, or any other recurring format you use.

Can I export my journal entries?

Yes. You can export your entries as PDF, CSV, Excel, or JSON files. This lets you create backups, share selected entries, or move your data to other platforms if needed.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes! The app runs natively on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later). Your entries sync automatically between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac via iCloud.

Can I write multiple entries per day?

Absolutely. Write as many entries as you want each day. Some people journal in the morning and evening, while others add notes throughout the day.

Do you show ads or track me?

No. We've completely removed all advertisements from the app. We don't track you across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data to advertisers.

Your data syncs via iCloud - just like Apple Notes and Photos. This means we don't need to know your email address, name, or any personal information. Your journal entries are stored on your devices and in your iCloud account, and they're completely private whether you use the free version or premium.

Do I need a subscription?

No — Feeltracker Daily Journal is free to download and no subscription is required. Writing, photos, templates, recurring entries, word analysis, search, charts, backups, exports, and iCloud sync are all free, and every user gets real AI features free every day (Quick Chat, Ask a Question, Voice Summary, Make Me Laugh, Calm Me Down, plus the Text and Item scanners) — with no ads, ever. An optional Pro subscription unlocks the most in-depth AI features (Deep Research, Theme Explorer, Sentiment Timeline, Doctor Summary, Pro Chat, and more), runs them on our most capable AI, and raises the generous daily limits.

Does Feeltracker Daily Journal work offline?

Yes — Feeltracker Daily Journal works offline for everything that matters: writing entries, attaching photos, searching, browsing your year, and viewing stats and charts all run on your device with no internet connection. Anything you write offline syncs to your other devices via iCloud the next time you go online. Cloud AI features need a connection, but if you switch on the on-device Apple Intelligence mode (Pro, on supported devices running iOS 26 or later), AI works offline too.

Can I import my old journal entries?

Yes — Feeltracker Daily Journal imports journal entries from an Excel spreadsheet. Download the import template from Settings, paste in a date, a time, and the text of each entry, then import the file and every row becomes a journal entry. You can also restore a previous backup, which brings back your entries and their photos together.

Can I lock my journal with Face ID?

Yes — you can lock Feeltracker Daily Journal with Face ID or Touch ID using the app lock built into iOS: press and hold the app icon on your Home Screen and choose Require Face ID. Settings links to the same instructions. Once it's on, nobody can open your journal without your face, fingerprint, or passcode.

What happens to my journal if I lose my phone?

Your journal is safe: Feeltracker Daily Journal syncs every entry and photo to your private iCloud account, so signing in on a new iPhone, iPad, or Mac brings it all back automatically. The app also writes an automatic backup roughly once a week (a ZIP of your entries and photos) that you can restore or export yourself at any time, and you can make a manual backup whenever you want one.
