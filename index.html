Common Questions

Do Feeltracker apps show ads or track me?

No. Feeltracker apps show no ads, use no ad networks, and never sell your data. We don't track you across other apps or websites (no IDFA or similar identifiers).

Your entries stay on your device and sync through your own iCloud account, just like Apple Notes and Photos, so we never need your name or email address. The one exception is if you choose to use a cloud AI feature: the data that feature needs is sent to OpenAI to produce your answer, as explained in our privacy policy. You can switch AI to on-device Apple Intelligence or turn it off entirely.

Do I need a subscription to use Feeltracker?

No — every Feeltracker app is free to download and free to use, with no ads. All tracking, charts, trends, Apple Health sync, camera scanning, and PDF/CSV/Excel/JSON exports are free, and every user also gets real AI features free every day: Quick Chat, Ask a Question, Voice Summary, Make Me Laugh, Calm Me Down, and a daily Quick version of the bigger reports.

An optional Pro subscription unlocks the full-depth AI features — Deep Research, Pattern Detective, Goal Assistant, Doctor Summary, Pro Chat, Voice Chat, Daily Insights, on-device Apple Intelligence, and the tools built for each app — and raises the daily limits.

What are the five Feeltracker apps?

Feeltracker is a suite of five health-tracking apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, made by Custom Arts. They are Blood Pressure Journal (systolic, diastolic, pulse, and MAP), Mood Journal (a 5-point mood scale with triggers and sentiment), Daily Journal (notes, photos, and recurring themes), Sleep Journal (sleep and wake times, plus a dream journal), and Weight Journal (weight, body fat, and BMI).

Each app works on its own, shares the same privacy model, and syncs through your own iCloud account. Use one or use all five.

Which devices do Feeltracker apps work on?

Feeltracker apps run on iPhone and iPad with iOS 16.0 or later, and on Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer). One purchase covers all your devices, and your entries sync automatically between them through your own iCloud account.

Apple Health integration is optional, and the on-device Apple Intelligence AI mode needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence on iOS 26 or later.
